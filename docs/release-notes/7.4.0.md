# Nishizumi Paints 7.4.0

Release date: 2026-11-02

This is the final release, offered by the in-app updater. It includes everything from the beta 2 build; the installer below installs over an existing installation and keeps your settings, catalog cache, and paints.

## New in 7.4.0

These changes were first published in beta 2.

### Trading Paints file names fixed

Decals downloaded by earlier versions were saved under a name iRacing does not read, so they never appeared on the car. The app now renames those old `decal_<id>.tga` files to `car_decal_<id>.tga` once at start-up, and writes new decals with that name. Custom paint extension paints are saved as `car_<id>_<ext>.tga`, with the same extension on their number, spec map and decal files. In team sessions, helmets are saved to the driver's own `helmet_<id>.tga` slot, because iRacing has no team helmet file. Car numbers are sent to Trading Paints as digits and dots only, so a number such as `A1` matches the paint for `1`.

### Diagnostics and recovery

- **View log** (Logs tab) opens `%APPDATA%\Nishizumi-Paints\logs\nishizumi-paints.log`. Unhandled errors, including errors from background threads and window callbacks, are written there with a traceback. The file keeps the last 31 days and is capped at 10 MB.
- If the Trading Paints app is running when this app starts, a warning explains that both apps write the same paint files and can overwrite each other. **Close Trading Paints** stops it, **Keep both** continues, and **Don't ask again** hides the warning.
- The **iRacing SDK** panel on the General tab checks that iRacing shares session data in memory (`irsdkEnableMem`). Press **Fix it** to enable it: the app backs up `app.ini`, sets `irsdkEnableMem=1`, and asks you to close iRacing first.
- If iRacing hands over session info that the app cannot read because of a driver name or design string, the app cleans those values and reads the session again instead of showing no session. If it still cannot be read, the raw session YAML is saved once per session under `%APPDATA%\Nishizumi-Paints\logs\yaml` for support.
- AI rosters are checked for duplicate driver names and car numbers and for characters Windows cannot use in a file name. The log lists what was found, and those characters are left out of generated file names.

### Field, session and paint panels

- The status bar at the bottom of the window shows the session **SOF**, the **Drivers** count, how many are **without livery**, **Files** done/total with a progress bar, and the **TP** and **SDK** connection states.
- The Session tab has a **Session conditions** panel with the sim date and time, air and track temperature, humidity, wind speed and direction, fog, skies, rain, track length, turns, altitude, city, country, and pit and pace speed limits. Use **Hide** and **Show** to collapse it.
- **Delete all downloaded paints…** (General tab) removes every paint downloaded for other drivers after showing the file count and total size. Your own paints, iRacing UI preview files and unrecognised files are kept.
- **Tags & forbidden…** (Session tab) opens **Tags & forbidden paints**, with the **Tagged drivers**, **Theme files** and **Forbidden paints** tabs. From there you can **Remove tag**, **Open themes folder**, and **Allow** a forbidden paint again.
- **League decal preview** (Showroom tab, League library): choose **League**, **Car**, **Class** and **Driver ID**, then press **Preview decal**. **Choose paint TGA…** previews the decal over your own paint, and **Export PNGs…** renders every league paint with its decal into PNG files.

### Faster downloads

- The app now asks Trading Paints about the whole field in one request instead of one request per driver; fields larger than 64 drivers are split into a few requests.
- Cars on track are painted first, cars in the garage next, and your own paints last.
- If a paint download fails on the main Trading Paints download server, the app tries the alternate server once before giving up.

7.4.0 adds a new **TP Download** tab, paint previews across the app, a showroom link preview, and a set of new tools for drivers, leagues and broadcasters.

## New: TP Download tab

Browse and download any Trading Paints member's car paints without being in a session.

- Type a member ID (your own is filled in) and press **Refresh** to list every car that member has a paint for.
- Select a car to preview it. The **Car** tab shows the 3-D render from the iRacing UI, and the **TGA** tab shows the paint with its decal and the decal on its own.
- **Trading Paints context:** pick the time of day (day/night), a team ID, a series ID or a league ID, and turn custom numbers on or off, to see the paint Trading Paints gives for that context (for example a team or league paint).
- **Download selected** or **Download all** saves the paints to `Documents\iRacing\paint` or a folder you choose.
- A **Friends** list: add drivers from the Session tab and download all their paints in one click.

Previews download the paint, unpack it, merge the decal and ask the iRacing UI for a render, so each car takes a few seconds.

## New: try a showroom paint on your car

Open a paint in the Trading Paints showroom, then drag the link from the browser's address bar onto the car preview (or paste it with **Ctrl+V** / **Paste showroom URL**). The paint is downloaded and previewed. If the preview shows your own car, it is also copied to `Documents\iRacing\paint\<car>`, so you see it in the iRacing UI model viewer and the paint booth; in the sim, press **Ctrl+R** to reload paints. Your existing decal is kept.

## New: previews in the Session tab

Selecting one driver shows their car, helmet and suit. Drivers without a livery are shown in their iRacing pattern, colours and number. **Export images** saves the pictures to `Pictures\iRacing`.

The 3-D previews need the iRacing UI to be open. Without it, the TGA preview is shown.

## New: Session tab tools

- **Forbid TP:** hide a driver's Trading Paints paint for that car (or their helmet or suit). The normal fallback is used instead (a random paint if you use the random fallback, otherwise the iRacing default). Click **Allow TP** to undo.
- **Copy to AI:** copy the selected drivers' car paints into the AI livery folder, with a note of where the paint came from.
- Right-click a driver to add them to your **friends**, give a **driver or team alias** (shown in the table), or **tag** them with a paint theme.

## New: league and broadcast tools

- **League library** (Showroom tab): keep league decals and per-driver league paints in one folder. In a session of that league they are applied automatically: the league decal for everyone (or for a car class) and a driver's own league decal are stacked on top of their Trading Paints decal, and a driver's league paint replaces their Trading Paints paint.
- **Paint themes (tags):** put themed paints in the themes folder and tag drivers with them from the Session tab. Tags can be copied to the clipboard and pasted back to share them.
- **Warning decals** (General tab): add a warning decal to drivers at or below an iRating and/or safety rating you choose. Off by default; rookies and AI are never marked.

## New: other options

- **Default spec maps** (General tab): drivers whose paint has no spec map get yours from a folder of `<car folder>.mip` files.
- **Car thumbnails** (General tab): save a rendered picture of your car, and optionally of every driver in the session.
- **Replay name** (General tab): define a replay name with tags like `{series}`, `{season}`, `{raceweek}`, `{track}` and `{car}`, with a live preview. It is copied to the clipboard when the race finishes, ready to paste into iRacing's save dialog.
- **iRacing folder sizes** (Logs tab): the size of the airosters, paint, replay, telemetry and videos folders and of the app's own folders, plus the free disk space (red below 10%).
- **Pause OneDrive and Dropbox** (General tab): stops them while the app runs and restarts them when it exits. Off by default.

## Fixed

- Files written by the new league, warning decal and default spec map features are removed at the end of the session like any other downloaded paint.
- A paint installed by a fallback without a spec map no longer keeps the previous driver's spec map.
- A damaged download (an error page, a cut-off file) no longer replaces a good paint.
- Your own car paint is kept when a session's paints are cleared, so the iRacing UI keeps showing it.
- The member ID is no longer changed while you watch someone else's replay.
- Closing the app while a download is stuck still cleans up the session's paints.
